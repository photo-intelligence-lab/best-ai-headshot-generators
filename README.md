## How this ranking was put together

We compared published pricing, photo counts, delivery times, refund terms and the sample galleries each company shows on its own site. All prices below were read from the official pricing pages at the time of writing (November 2026), in US dollars. Promotional prices are shown as they appeared; where a company lists a higher "regular" price, we mention it.

We didn't run lab tests or invent scores. The ranking is an opinion built on four things you can check yourself:

- **Cost per usable photo.** A headshot session is a numbers game. Some outputs will have odd ears or a tie that melts into the shirt. More photos per dollar means more chances at a great one.

- **Pricing model.** One-time packs vs. recurring subscriptions.

- **Range.** Can you only get grey-backdrop corporate shots, or can you also make a speaker photo, a casual team-page portrait, a dating photo?

- **Safety net.** Refunds, redos, or support that keeps going until you're happy.

## 1. Narkis.ai: the most photos for the least money

Narkis works the way the better tools in this category all work now: you upload a set of selfies, it trains a personal AI model of your face, and then you generate photos from that model. The difference is in the volume and the flexibility.

The Starter pack is $27 one-time and covers up to 200 AI photos. Premium is $54 for up to 500 photos plus HD AI video, and Pro is $108 for up to 1,100 photos with HD video and early access to new features. Packs never expire, so you can make a LinkedIn photo today and come back in six months for a conference bio shot without paying again. At $0.135 per photo on the entry tier, it's the cheapest per-photo option in this comparison by a wide margin. Monthly and yearly subscriptions exist too, but you don't need one.

The other reason it ranks first is range. The style menu covers LinkedIn, corporate, executive and power-suit headshots, but you aren't locked into them. You can write your own prompt, try on different outfits virtually, relight a photo that came out too flat, or turn any image into a short AI video. That matters because a lot of people buying a "headshot" really need three or four different photos: one for LinkedIn, one for the company site, one slightly more relaxed for a podcast guest page.

The first photos arrive in about seven minutes. If the results don't look right, Narkis's support team keeps working with you until you have photos you're happy with. The company was founded in 2023 in Luxembourg and runs its site in eight languages.

You can see the headshot styles here: [Narkis AI headshots](https://narkis.ai/ai-headshots).

**Best for:** individuals who want lots of options, people who need more than one kind of photo, anyone who hates subscriptions.

**Watch out for:** no free preview before you pay. You're relying on the guarantee instead.

## 2. HeadshotPro: the team rollout pick

HeadshotPro's individual plans are $29 for 30 photos, $39 for 50 and $59 for 70, all one-time. The Basic tier takes about two hours; the top tier comes back in about fifteen minutes.

Its real strength is the team product. Plans start at $19.50 per person, and the company advertises SOC 2, SSO and an API, which is what an HR or IT department will ask about before approving a vendor. If you're ordering matching headshots for 80 employees, that paperwork matters more than per-photo cost.

For a single person, though, 30 photos at $29 is thin next to the 200 you get from Narkis at $27. HeadshotPro offers a money-back promise if the photos don't look like you, plus one free redo.

## 3. Aragon AI: the familiar corporate look

Aragon is probably the most recognised name in AI headshots. At the time of writing it was running a 20%-off banner: Basic $35 (list $44) for 40 photos in 45 minutes, Standard $45 for 60 in 30 minutes, and Executive $75 for 100 in 15 minutes.

The sample gallery leans hard into polished corporate portraits, and that's what it does well. Per photo, you pay $0.75 to $0.88, which is several times what Narkis charges. There's no free trial; Aragon offers a free redo or a refund if you haven't downloaded the photos yet.

## 4. BetterPic: try before you buy

BetterPic is the only tool here that lets you see real output before paying: up to 10 free 4K headshots, though the free queue can take a while. Paid plans are Basic $35 for 20 photos, Pro $39 for 60 and Expert $79 for 120, with delivery between one and two hours. There's also a teams plan at $49 per seat and an API at $299 per month.

The 4K output is a genuine plus if you need print-quality images. The entry tier is the worst value in this comparison at $1.75 per photo, so if you do buy, the Pro tier makes more sense.

## 5. Secta Labs: the style browser

Secta's Professional plan was $49 one-time at the time of writing (list $99) for 200+ HD photos, delivered in under an hour. It's the only competitor that comes close to Narkis on volume. The draw is the style library, which runs into the hundreds, and a "Remix" feature for editing results.

At roughly $0.25 per photo it's good value, just not as good as $27 for 200. It also only covers headshots, so if you later want a dating or travel photo from the same model, you'd need a different tool. 30-day money-back guarantee, custom pricing for teams.

## 6. StudioShot: built by photographers

StudioShot pitches itself as made by professional photographers, and its review count (4.8 from 11,000+ reviews, per its own site) suggests a lot of satisfied buyers. Pricing we saw: Essential $29.25 for 40 photos, Pro $36.86 for 80, Signature $43.88 for 120, with delivery from under three hours down to under one. Those odd-cent prices may be regional conversions.

The Signature tier at about $0.37 per photo is decent. The entry tier is less so. 100% money-back guarantee.

## 7. Try It On AI: one selfie, big discounts

Try It On AI advertises "starting from $17" and needs only one selfie to get going. But the full tier list wasn't shown publicly (its pricing page returned a 404 when we checked), so value is hard to judge. One selfie also gives the model less to learn your face from.

## 8. PhotoAI: powerful, but a subscription

PhotoAI is a broad generator, not a headshot specialist. It does headshots, dating packs, video and "AI influencer" content. Plans are billed annually: Starter $9/mo ($99/yr) for 5 photos a month, Pro $29/mo ($349/yr) for 100, Max $49/mo ($599/yr) for 300, Ultra $99/mo ($1,199/yr) for 1,000. We couldn't see monthly-billing prices.

If you produce content every week, that can make sense. For one headshot, it's $99 to $349 up front versus $27.

## Getting good results from any of them

The selfies you feed in matter more than the tool. Upload 15 to 20 photos from different days, in different light, with different expressions. Drop filtered shots, sunglasses and group photos. When results arrive, keep the five that look most like you on a good day.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI headshot generator in 2026?

For most people, Narkis.ai. It costs $27 one-time for up to 200 photos, which works out to $0.135 per photo, and covers far more styles than headshot-only tools. HeadshotPro is a strong pick for company teams.

### Are AI headshot generators worth paying for?

For most professional uses, yes. A studio session commonly costs much more than any tool here, and you get dozens or hundreds of variations instead of a handful. The trade-off is that you need to pick carefully, because not every AI output will be perfect.

### Which AI headshot tool has a free trial?

BetterPic was the only one in this comparison offering free output: up to 10 4K headshots. The others rely on money-back guarantees, redos or, in Narkis's case, support that keeps helping until you're satisfied.

### Is a subscription or a one-time payment better for headshots?

One-time, unless you produce new images every week. Narkis, HeadshotPro, Aragon, BetterPic, Secta and StudioShot all sell one-time packs. PhotoAI is subscription-only and billed annually.

### How long does an AI headshot take?

Narkis delivers first photos in about seven minutes. Aragon's tiers range from 15 to 45 minutes, Secta says under an hour, and HeadshotPro and BetterPic can take up to two hours on entry plans.

### Can recruiters tell a photo is AI-generated?

A good one, usually not, as long as it actually looks like you. Skip anything with over-smoothed skin, odd teeth or nonsense backgrounds.
